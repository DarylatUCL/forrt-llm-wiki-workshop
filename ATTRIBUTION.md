# Attribution

`demo-vault/sources/` holds fifteen published papers, one folder each: the publisher's PDF, a markdown conversion of it, and the figures extracted during conversion. The papers belong to their authors. They are redistributed here under the licences their publishers attach to them, listed below.

Changes made: each PDF was converted to markdown by an automated tool, and its figures were saved as separate image files. The PDFs are unaltered. The conversions may contain errors, such as garbled tables or a sentence split around a figure. The PDF is the authoritative copy.

The wiki pages under `demo-vault/wiki/` and `demo-vault-after/wiki/` summarise and quote these papers. They are agent output and are not endorsed by the papers' authors.

Licences were confirmed on 3 September 2026 against each publisher's own statement; `demo-vault/corpus.md` records how.
